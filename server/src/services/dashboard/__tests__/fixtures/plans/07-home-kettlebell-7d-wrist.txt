Monday
Warmup: 5 mins (Arm circles, cat-cow, bird-dogs, bodyweight squats).
Main lifts:
- KB Swings: 5 x 20 (60s rest)
- Pull-ups: 5 x Max (2 reps short of failure) (90s rest)
Accessories:
- KB Goblet Squats: 3 x 12 (60s rest)
- KB Single Arm Rows: 3 x 10/side (Neutral wrist, 60s rest)
Finisher:
- 5-minute AMRAP: 10 KB Swings + 5 Burpees (Step-back to save wrists).

Tuesday
Warmup: 5 mins (Jumping jacks, mountain climbers, wrist circles).
Main lifts:
- KB Clean & Press: 5 x 8/side (Focus on vertical forearm, 90s rest)
- KB Goblet Lunges: 4 x 10/side (60s rest)
Accessories:
- Hanging Leg Raises: 3 x 12 (60s rest)
- KB Floor Press: 3 x 12 (Keep wrists stacked over elbows, 60s rest)
Finisher:
- 4 Rounds: 30s Mountain Climbers, 30s KB Swings, 30s Shadow Boxing (30s rest).

Wednesday
Warmup: 5 mins (Hip openers, light lunges, plank hold).
Main lifts:
- KB Single-Leg RDL: 4 x 10/side (Focus on hinge pattern, 60s rest)
- Chin-ups: 4 x Max (Underhand grip often easier on elbows, 90s rest)
Accessories:
- KB Halo: 3 x 10/side (Light weight, 45s rest)
- KB Russian Twists: 3 x 20 total (45s rest)
Finisher:
- 8-minute EMOM: Odd mins - 15 Swings; Even mins - 10 Goblet Squats.

Thursday
Warmup: 5 mins (Dynamic stretching, thoracic spine rotations).
Main lifts:
- KB Front Squats: 5 x 10 (90s rest)
- Pull-up Bar Dead Hangs: 3 x 45-60s (Focus on shoulder engagement, 60s rest)
Accessories:
- KB Windmills: 3 x 5/side (Light weight, focus on mobility, 60s rest)
- Push-ups: 3 x Max (Perform on KB handles to keep wrists neutral, 60s rest)
Finisher:
- 50 KB Swings for time (Break as needed).

Friday
Warmup: 5 mins (High knees, butt kicks, arm swings).
Main lifts:
- KB High Pulls: 5 x 10/side (Better for elbows than snatches, 60s rest)
- KB Thrusters: 4 x 12 (90s rest)
Accessories:
- L-Sits or Hanging Knee Tucks: 3 x 30s (60s rest)
- KB Suitcase Carry: 3 x 40 meters/side (60s rest)
Finisher:
- Tabata (8 rounds of 20s work / 10s rest): Alternating KB Swings.

Saturday
Warmup: 5 mins (Bodyweight circuit: Squat, lunge, plank).
Main lifts:
- KB Alternating Cleans: 5 x 10/side (Focus on "taming the arc," 60s rest)
- Pull-ups: 4 x Max (Use varied grip widths, 90s rest)
Accessories:
- KB Lateral Lunges: 3 x 10/side (60s rest)
- Plank with KB Pull-throughs: 3 x 12 total (Keep hips level, 60s rest)
Finisher:
- 10-minute AMRAP: 5 Pull-ups, 10 Push-ups (on handles), 15 Air Squats.

Sunday
Warmup: 5 mins (Full body mobility flow).
Main lifts:
- KB Deadlifts: 5 x 15 (Focus on explosive glute drive, 60s rest)
- Hanging Windshield Wipers or Knee Tucks: 4 x 10 (60s rest)
Accessories:
- KB Overhead Carry: 3 x 30 meters/side (Neutral wrist, 60s rest)
- Bodyweight Glute Bridges: 3 x 20 (45s rest)
Finisher:
- 15 minutes of Low-Intensity Steady State (LISS) (Brisk walk or light shadow boxing).

Coach Notes:
- To manage wrist/elbow pain, perform push-ups while holding the kettlebell handles; this keeps the wrist in a neutral, stacked position.
- If the "flip" of the kettlebell in cleans or snatches hurts your wrist, prioritize KB High Pulls or Swings instead.
- Grip strength is vital for advanced kettlebell work. If your elbows flare up, reduce the volume of pull-ups and focus on the Dead Hangs to build tendon resilience.
- For fat loss, prioritize intensity during finishers while maintaining strict form during main lifts.
- Hydrate and prioritize 7-8 hours of sleep, as a 7-day schedule requires high-quality recovery. Check in with your joint health mid-week; if pain increases, take an extra rest day.

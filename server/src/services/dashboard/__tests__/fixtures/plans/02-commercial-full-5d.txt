Monday
Warmup: 5-10 mins dynamic stretching (leg swings, gate openers, bodyweight squats).
Main lifts:
- Back Squats: 3 x 5 (Rest: 3 mins)
- Conventional Deadlifts: 2 x 5 (Rest: 3-5 mins)
Accessories:
- Leg Press: 3 x 10-12 (Rest: 90s)
- Seated Calf Raises: 3 x 15 (Rest: 60s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Wall Sits: 3 x 45 seconds (Rest: 45s)

Tuesday
Warmup: 5-10 mins arm circles, band pull-aparts, and light cat-cow stretches.
Main lifts:
- Barbell Bench Press: 3 x 5 (Rest: 3 mins)
- Pendlay Rows: 3 x 6-8 (Rest: 2 mins)
Accessories:
- Standing Overhead Press: 3 x 8 (Rest: 90s)
- Pull-ups (weighted if possible): 3 x Max reps (Rest: 90s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Battle Ropes: 5 rounds of 30s work / 30s rest.

Wednesday
Warmup: 5 mins foam rolling (hips, lats, and upper back).
Main lifts:
- Active Recovery: 20 mins Lap Swimming or Low-intensity Stationary Bike (Zone 2).
Accessories:
- Full Body Mobility Circuit: 3 rounds of Thoracic Rotations (10/side), 90/90 Hip Switches (10/side), and Bird-Dogs (12 reps).
Finisher/conditioning:
- 10 mins in Sauna or Steam Room for recovery.

Thursday
Warmup: 5 mins glute bridges, bird-dogs, and unweighted lunges.
Main lifts:
- Romanian Deadlifts: 3 x 10 (Rest: 2 mins)
- Goblet Squats (Kettlebell): 3 x 12 (Rest: 90s)
Accessories:
- Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 x 10 per leg (Rest: 90s)
- Leg Curls (Lying or Seated): 3 x 12-15 (Rest: 60s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Kettlebell Swings: 4 x 20 reps (Rest: 60s)

Friday
Warmup: 5 mins light rowing machine and face pulls with light band.
Main lifts:
- Incline Dumbbell Bench Press: 3 x 10 (Rest: 90s)
- Wide Grip Lat Pulldowns: 3 x 10-12 (Rest: 90s)
Accessories:
- Dumbbell Lateral Raises: 3 x 15 (Rest: 60s)
- Cable Bicep Curls: 3 x 12 (Superset with)
- Cable Tricep Pushdowns: 3 x 12 (Rest: 60s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Treadmill Intervals: 6 rounds of 30s sprint / 60s walk.

Saturday
Warmup: 5 mins jump rope or jumping jacks.
Main lifts:
- Farmer’s Carries: 4 x 40 meters (Rest: 2 mins)
- Sled Pushes/Pulls: 4 rounds of 20 meters (Rest: 2 mins)
Accessories:
- Box Jumps: 3 x 8 (Rest: 60s)
- Hanging Leg Raises: 3 x 12 (Rest: 60s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Rowing Machine: 1000m for time.

Sunday
Warmup: None.
Main lifts: Full Rest.
Accessories: Optional 20-minute walk outdoors.
Finisher/conditioning: Focused stretching on tight areas (Hamstrings/Chest).

Coach Notes:
- Progressions: Aim to increase the weight on your "Main Lifts" by 2.5kg to 5kg every two weeks to ensure strength gains.
- Energy Management: On Wednesday, keep the intensity very low; it is meant to flush out soreness, not create more fatigue.
- Nutrition: Since the goal is lean strength, focus on a high-protein diet (approx 1.6g to 2g per kg of body weight) to support muscle repair.
- Recovery: Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep. If you feel excessively fatigued, reduce the "Accessories" sets by one for that session.

Coach Notes:
- Focus on "Rate of Perceived Exertion" (RPE). Aim for a 7/10 effort during the main cardio intervals; you should be breathing hard but able to speak in short sentences.
- As a beginner, prioritize form over speed. On kettlebell swings, ensure the power comes from your hips, not your arms.
- Hydration is key for cardio-heavy sessions. Aim for 500ml of water during the 45-minute window.
- If the treadmill feels too high-impact for your joints, swap it for the elliptical or stationary bike using the same interval timing.
- Proper recovery involves 7-9 hours of sleep, especially on training days, to allow your cardiovascular system to adapt.

Monday
Warmup: 5 minutes of dynamic movement (arm circles, leg swings, cat-cow, and neck rolls).
Main lifts:
- Bodyweight Squats: 3 x 12 (Rest: 60s)
- Standard or Incline Push-ups: 3 x 8-10 (Rest: 60s)
Accessories:
- Glute Bridges: 3 x 15 (Rest: 45s)
- Plank: 3 x 30-45 seconds (Rest: 45s)
Finisher/conditioning: 
- Mountain Climbers: 3 x 30 seconds (Rest: 30s)

Tuesday
Rest or 20-minute light walk.

Wednesday
Warmup: 5 minutes (Shoulder taps, lunges with a twist, and jumping jacks).
Main lifts:
- Reverse Lunges: 3 x 10 per leg (Rest: 60s)
- Prone Y-Extensions (Lie on stomach, lift chest/arms in 'Y' shape): 3 x 12 (Rest: 60s)
Accessories:
- Bird-Dogs: 3 x 10 per side (Rest: 45s)
- Side Plank: 3 x 20 seconds per side (Rest: 45s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- High Knees: 3 x 30 seconds (Rest: 30s)

Thursday
Rest or 20-minute light walk.

Friday
Warmup: 5 minutes (Gate openers, torso twists, and toe touches).
Main lifts:
- Bulgarian Split Squats (back foot on a chair/couch): 3 x 8 per leg (Rest: 60s)
- Pike Push-ups (hips high in 'V' shape): 3 x 8 (Rest: 60s)
Accessories:
- Single-Leg Bodyweight Deadlift (focus on balance): 3 x 10 per leg (Rest: 45s)
- Lying Leg Raises: 3 x 12 (Rest: 45s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- Burpees (step-back version for beginners): 3 x 10 reps (Rest: 45s)

Saturday
Active Recovery: 15-20 minutes of full-body mobility or yoga-style stretching.

Sunday
Full Rest.

Coach Notes:
- Progression: As a beginner, focus on form first. Once a movement feels easy, slow down the "negative" (the lowering phase) to 3 seconds to increase intensity without adding weight.
- Push-up Subs: If standard push-ups are too difficult, place your hands on a kitchen counter or a sturdy table to reduce the load.
- Energy Tip: Keep the 30-minute sessions high-intent. If you finish early, add one extra set to the main lifts.
- Recovery: Ensure you are sleeping 7-8 hours and drinking at least 2-3 liters of water daily to support lean strength gains.
- Back Health: For the Prone Y-Extensions, keep your gaze on the floor to maintain a neutral spine.

Monday
Warmup: 5 mins - Arm circles, cat-cow on mat, light bird-dogs, and 2 sets of 10 push-ups (knees or bench).
Main lifts:
- DB Incline Bench Press: 3 x 10-12 (Rest 60s)
- DB Standing Overhead Press: 3 x 10 (Rest 60s)
Accessories:
- DB Lateral Raises: 3 x 15 (Rest 45s)
- DB Tricep Overhead Extension: 3 x 12 (Rest 45s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- 5 Rounds: 30 seconds Shadow Boxing / 30 seconds Rest.

Tuesday
Warmup: 5 mins - Glute bridges (bodyweight), 90/90 hip flow on mat, and ankle rotations.
Main lifts:
- DB Romanian Deadlifts: 3 x 12 (Focus on hinge, keep shins vertical to protect knee) (Rest 90s)
- DB Box Squats (Sit to bench and stand): 3 x 12 (Rest 90s)
Accessories:
- DB Weighted Glute Bridges (Shoulders on mat): 3 x 15 (Rest 60s)
- Standing Calf Raises: 3 x 20 (Rest 45s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- 3 Rounds: 1-minute Plank / 30 seconds Side Plank (each side). No rest between rounds.

Wednesday
Warmup: 5 mins - Full body mobility flow on yoga mat.
Main lifts:
- Active Recovery: 20-30 minute brisk walk or steady-state movement.
Accessories:
- Light Yoga/Stretching: 15 mins focusing on hip flexors and quads.
Finisher/conditioning:
- None. Focus on recovery.

Thursday
Warmup: 5 mins - Scapular shrugs, DB "Y" raises (very light), and T-spine rotations on mat.
Main lifts:
- DB Chest-Supported Row (Bench at 45 degrees): 3 x 10-12 (Rest 60s)
- DB Single-Arm Row: 3 x 12 per side (Rest 60s)
Accessories:
- DB Bicep Curls: 3 x 12 (Rest 45s)
- DB Rear Delt Flyes: 3 x 15 (Rest 45s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- 8-minute AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible): 10 DB Renegade Rows (no pushup) + 20 Mountain Climbers (slow and controlled).

Friday
Warmup: 5 mins - High knees (low impact), arm swings, and bodyweight squats to bench.
Main lifts:
- DB Goblet Box Squats: 3 x 15 (Rest 60s)
- DB Floor Press: 3 x 12 (Rest 60s)
Accessories:
- DB Alternating Reverse Lunges: 3 x 10 per leg (Step back cautiously, maintain vertical front shin) (Rest 60s)
- DB Suitcase Carry: 3 x 30 seconds per side (Rest 30s)
Finisher/conditioning:
- EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute) for 10 minutes:
- Odd Minutes: 15 DB Swings (hinge focus)
- Even Minutes: 10 "No-Jump" Burpees (Step back instead of jump).

Saturday
Warmup: 5 mins - Dynamic stretching.
Main lifts:
- Optional: Light outdoor activity or 30 mins of steady-state cardio.
Accessories:
- Core Circuit: 3 rounds of 15 Leg Raises and 20 Russian Twists.
Finisher/conditioning:
- None.

Sunday
Warmup: None.
Main lifts:
- Full Rest Day.
Accessories:
- Foam rolling or static stretching for 15 minutes.
Finisher/conditioning:
- None.

Coach Notes:
- Knee Safety: If any squatting or lunging causes sharp pain, reduce the range of motion (don't go as deep) or stick to the Romanian Deadlift and Glute Bridge movements which are more "posterior chain" dominant and knee-friendly.
- Tempo: Control the eccentric (lowering) phase of all lifts for 2-3 seconds. This builds strength and stability without needing extremely heavy weights.
- Fat Loss: Focus on maintaining a high heart rate during finishers. If the 45-minute mark is approaching, prioritize the main lifts and trim the accessories.
- Recovery: Ensure you are sleeping 7-8 hours. Since you have a knee limitation, prioritize icing or compression if you experience inflammation after lower body days.

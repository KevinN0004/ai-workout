Coach Notes:
- Shoulder Safety: During all upper body movements, stop immediately if you feel sharp pain. Use the lightest resistance bands available. Mobility is about quality of movement, not intensity.
- Breathing: Inhale through the nose and exhale through the mouth during holds. This triggers the parasympathetic nervous system for better recovery.
- Consistency: Since this is a 20-minute beginner plan, focus on hitting the two training days perfectly. If you feel good, you can add 5 minutes of light walking before starting.
- Progression: As your shoulder feels more stable, slowly increase the "time under tension" rather than adding heavier bands.

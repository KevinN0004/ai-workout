Sunday

Finisher/conditioning: 30 min Low-Intensity Steady State (LISS) walk outdoors or on treadmill.

Coach Notes:
To protect your lower back, prioritize "bracing" your core before every lift as if someone is about to punch you in the stomach. Since you are advanced, focus on the Mind-Muscle Connection rather than just moving heavy weight, especially on leg days where spinal loading is a risk. Avoid conventional deadlifts from the floor; the DB RDLs and Rack Pulls (if substituted) are safer for your history. Ensure you get 7-8 hours of sleep and prioritize protein intake (approx. 1g per lb of body weight) to support lean strength gains. If you feel "tight" in the lower back, swap any standing movement for a seated/supported version.

Coach Notes:
- Since we are focusing on hip recovery, the pool is your best tool. The buoyancy reduces joint loading while providing 360-degree resistance.
- During lap swimming, if the hip feels "pinched" during a breaststroke kick, switch to a flutter kick or use a pull buoy to keep the legs stationary.
- In the Mobility Zone, focus on "active" range of motion. Do not push into sharp pain; stay within a 3/10 discomfort range.
- Hydration is key even in the pool. You are still sweating, even if you can't feel it.
- Use the 1-day session to set the tone for the week. On "Rest" days, a 5-minute walk or gentle stretching at home is encouraged but not mandatory.
